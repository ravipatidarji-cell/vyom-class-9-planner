    Padhne ke niyam

    • Book band pehle: revision ka matlab dobara padhna nahi, pehle khud likh ke ya bol ke yaad karna, phir book se match.
    • Revision cycle: agle din → 7 din baad → 21 din baad → monthly test. App spaced revision khud lagata hai.
    • Mistakes notebook: har galti ki category — concept / recall / careless / time. Hafte me ek hi galti dobara nahi honi chahiye.
    • Homework pehle: school homework usi subject ke block me count; alag se extra load nahi.
    • 95%+ ka signal: Feb ke mock papers me lagataar 90%+ aana.
    • Neend aur swimming kaatni nahi. Thaka hua dimaag yaad nahi rakhta.

    School se confirm karna hai

    • English: Unit 1–4 ko old (first term) maana hai, 5–8 ko naya.
    • Sanskrit ke 11 Path + Parishisht pages 1–37 tracker aur 20-minute study/revision slots me add hain; annual exam inclusion school se confirm karna pending hai.
    • AI (417): baaki units CBSE 417 ke standard units maane hain; school ka annual syllabus aane par badlenge.
    • Pre-annual (8–20 Dec) ka exact syllabus aur date sheet.
    • Annual (1–17 Mar) ki date sheet — exam-days ke tasks usi hisaab se.
